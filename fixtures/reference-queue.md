# Illustrative queue at a service desk

This is a simplified teaching scenario, not measured business data.

- People arrive, join a waiting line, receive service, and then leave.
- Each service desk handles one person at a time in this illustration.
- During a period when more people join the line than finish service, the line grows.
- During a period when more people finish service than join, the line shrinks.
- Opening another desk can help if it serves the same kind of request and increases the total rate of completed service.
- A second desk does not guarantee the line will disappear. Combined service must exceed arrivals for an existing line to shrink.
- Explain the balance between arrivals and completed service qualitatively. Do not invent measured statistics, waiting times, or rates for a real business.
